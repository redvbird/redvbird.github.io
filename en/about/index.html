About us

A small, dependable IT team that foreign companies in Shanghai have trusted for over a decade.

Since 2014, our team has provided IT support to companies across Shanghai. Over those years the same people, the same standards and the same clients have carried through — today we operate as Shanghai Sitongsi Information Technology, but the team and the way we work are unchanged.

We're deliberately small and hands-on. When you call, you reach the people who actually do the work — not a ticket queue. That's why so much of our growth has come from existing clients referring us to others: the relationships last because the service does.

We've spent years supporting foreign-invested companies — from the US, the Netherlands, Thailand and beyond — as well as design studios and trading firms. Our engineers are fluent in English and familiar with how international companies operate, from communication style to compliance expectations. For a foreign company in Shanghai, that means IT support that simply understands you.

What we stand for
