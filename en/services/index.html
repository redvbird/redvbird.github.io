Our services

Everything your office IT needs, from one team that already knows your setup. Choose a full managed plan or call us when you need us.

IT support & managed services

Day-to-day support for your team's computers and network: desktop and laptop troubleshooting (Mac and Windows), network and Wi-Fi management, email and domain account administration, patching, antivirus and backup. Available on a monthly or annual contract so you get a reliable IT function without hiring in-house staff.

Servers & network hosting

Setup, maintenance and monitoring of your servers and network equipment: firewall and switch configuration, VPN, server room inspection, equipment maintenance and IDC hosting. We keep the systems your business depends on running reliably, around the clock.

Hardware procurement & maintenance

Specifying and sourcing computers, monitors, printers and network gear at transparent prices — no hidden markup. We handle delivery, setup and both in- and out-of-warranty repairs, so your hardware stays managed from purchase to retirement.

Structured cabling & office setup

Design and installation of network cabling, CCTV, access control and video-conferencing systems. Setting up a new office or relocating? We deliver the whole thing end-to-end, so everyone can plug in and start working on day one.
